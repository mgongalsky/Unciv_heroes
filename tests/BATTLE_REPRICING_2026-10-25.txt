Scope: tactical recruitment prices in Civ V - Gods & Kings/Units.json.

Method
Use production BattleManager, StandaloneBattleSetup and AI, seven evenly
split stacks per army (fewer when the soldier count is below seven).
Budget: 500. Field: default 14x8, terrain seed 42, default climates.
Luck and morale probabilities: 0.15. Limits: 1000 turns, 100 without progress.

First measure all 25 directed matchups at the original budget-scale attacker
counts: Peasant 500, Archer 125, Spearman 62, Swordsman 45, Horseman 31.
Search defender counts 1..5000 with seeds 200..207.
Fit log(price_attacker)-log(price_defender)=log(defendersPerAttacker)
over the 20 non-mirror pairs by least squares, anchoring Peasant=1.
The fit gave Archer 4.2928, Spearman 9.5172, Swordsman 12.5401,
Horseman 15.9010. Boundary estimates assume a decreasing crossing.

Limits
Results describe homogeneous armies, this budget, one terrain setup and the
current AI. They do not establish balance for mixed armies, other budgets,
other terrain or human play. Strong matchup counters and role asymmetry remain.

Reproduction
Run BattleRepricingSevenStacksTest.measureCurrentBudgetScaleBoundaries for
fresh boundary measurements. It reads current JSON prices to choose army sizes,
so rerunning after this update uses different attacker counts.
BattleRepricingValidationTest.compareEqualBudgetPrices currently compares the
intermediate and selected candidates using the documented validation seeds.
Historical candidate inputs and results remain in their respective reports.
